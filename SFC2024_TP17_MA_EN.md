### **C9: Loading (Intermediate)** 

A counter that counts numbers from 10 to 0 is required, which meets the following requirements: 

1. You can stop the countdown when you click on the counter, as shown in the video **loading.mp4** . 

2. When you stop the counter, the circle will become larger and change to #E35B46 hexadecimal color with border line 1px # AB4C3E hexadecimal color, and the following text will also be displayed: “Don't Hesitate”, as shown in the video **loading.mp4.** 

3. You can resume the countdown by double clicking on the counter, remember that time resumes where it stopped and the text: “Don't Hesitate” should disappear, as shown in the video **loading.mp4.**

### **C10: Turntable (Hard)** 

Create a fun animation of a record player so the user can listen to the popular song of the week. 

The turntable must meet the following requirements: 

1. When the user presses the platter, the song plays, as shown in the video **turntable.mp4.** 

2. When the song is played the turntable activates the animation and the tone-arm controller moves above the platter, as shown in the video **turntable.mp4.** 

3. The **OFF** button stops the song completely, as shown in the video **turntable.mp4.** 

4. The volume controller will be a minimum of 0 and a maximum of 1. The user can vary the volume of the song, as shown in the video **turntable.mp4.** 

5. If the user presses the **>>** button (next button), it changes the other song, as shown in the video **turntable.mp4.** 

6. If the song ends, the animation of the tone-arm its original state, as shown in the video **turntable.mp4** .
